Outcomes achieved at Thinkific

From program setup to retention.

Conversion, program creation, completion, and retention come from different measurement windows. Read them as a ladder of signals, not one causal chain.

  1. Leading

    Customers start and finish setting up programs

    Program-creation conversion 63.8% → 68.9% · new active programs ~10% month over month in the month after launch, still rising.

    Why it matters next: recertification gives admins a commercial reason to create certification programs.

  2. Lagging

    Customers turn on recurring certification

    80% recertification enablement in beta

    Why it matters next: programs with renewals built in keep learners engaged and recertifying.

  3. Success metric

    Customers succeeding, not just adopting

    Active programs completed within 60 days: ~30% → 45% month over month. I picked program health over raw adoption because a created program only creates value when learners complete it.

    Why it matters next: recurring value for learners gives customers a reason to renew and expand.

  4. North star

    Retention

    Contributed to +4 points net revenue retention for target certification customers (93% → 97%, quarter over quarter; the segment had sat flat at ~93%).

    Commercial proof: deals lost mainly to certification gaps were cut in half (12 → 6, $143K) after recertification launched.

  5. Guardrail

    Setup stayed simple while features were added

    Certificate designer time ~230 → ~190 seconds.

10% → 35%

Dashboard click-through

+25 percentage points within two weeks of release, among all certification users with dashboard access. Measured separately from the program and retention metrics; the link didn’t cause all of that growth.
