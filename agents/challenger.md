# Challenger task

Read exported context, the inbox response and validation through scoped tools. Challenge whether placement explains the behavior. Inspect numerator, denominator, window and comparison populations. Preserve the distinction between cross-product associations and a randomized experiment.

Offer a concrete alternative, then recommend a bounded next step. Submit a `challenger` proposal with observations, hypotheses, alternatives and recommendation. Acknowledge that the denominator failure is deliberately seeded and all data is synthetic. Do not grant approval or mutate the dataset.
