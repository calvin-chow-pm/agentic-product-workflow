Outcomes achieved at Thinkific

From activation to retention.

Signals from different measurement windows — a ladder, not one causal chain.

  1. Leading

    Customers start and finish setting up programs

    Program-creation conversion 63.8% → 68.9% · new active programs ~10% month over month in the month after launch, still rising.

  2. ↑Recertification gives admins a commercial reason to create certification programs.
  3. Lagging

    Customers turn on recurring certification

    80% recertification enablement in beta

  4. ↑Programs with renewals built in keep learners engaged and recertifying.
  5. Success metric

    Customers succeeding, not just adopting

    Active programs completed within 60 days: ~30% → 45% month over month. I picked program health over raw adoption because a created program only creates value when learners complete it.

  6. ↑Recurring value for learners gives customers a reason to renew and expand their plans.
  7. North star

    Retention

    Contributed to +4 pts NRR for target certification customers (93% → 97%, QoQ; the segment had sat flat at ~93%).

    Deals lost mainly to certification gaps were cut in half (12 → 6, $143K) after recertification launched.
